Sportfolio is a virtual-currency sports strategy game. It does not offer real-money gambling, sports betting, cash-out, or redeemable prizes.

Core gameplay lets users trade virtual player shares, assign scouts, and use daily boost slots tied to live game performance. iOS-native builds intentionally disable external checkout and purchase sync flows for digital goods while Apple in-app purchase support is being finalized.

Reviewer access should use active Sportfolio test credentials supplied in App Store Connect. The backend is available at https://www.sportfolio.market during review.
