# Country availability

## Initial recommendation

Select **United States** only for the initial submission.

Sportfolio's publisher, product operations, support process, legal pages, and current sports focus are presently centered on the United States. Limiting the initial launch avoids implying legal, support, language, or product readiness in countries that have not been reviewed.

## Expansion gate

Add another country or region only after confirming:

- Sportfolio is available and supported there;
- the publisher can provide reasonable user support;
- privacy and terms are appropriate for the jurisdiction;
- sports-data and authentication dependencies work there;
- the product's virtual fantasy-sports mechanics do not require additional disclosures;
- the marketplace listing and support materials are understandable to the intended users.
