# Start with your cash balance

Every new account begins with play money. Your first job is deciding how much to keep liquid and how much to deploy into player shares, liquidity positions, or boost-ready inventory.

# Learn the core loops

Sportfolio has a few recurring loops that matter more than everything else:

- trade player shares in AMM-backed pools
- assign scouts to earn shares over time
- condense shares into higher-power inventory
- use daily and community boosts around game windows
- manage vesting claims and redeployment

# Build a sensible first routine

For most users, the best first routine is simple:

1. Buy a small basket of players you actually want to track.
2. Assign scouts to conviction names instead of spreading too thin.
3. Keep some cash idle so you can react to news or price swings.
4. Review your boost slots before lock.

# Use the agent as an operator

The Sportfolio agent can explain mechanics, review your current setup, research current news with hosted web search, and stage supported actions for confirmation. It is strongest when you ask for a specific review or a concrete action.
