# Scout assignments

Scouts are assignable units that work for your account. You place them on players, and the system distributes shares over time based on participation.

# The important mechanic

Distribution is time-weighted. The system cares about scout-minutes, not just whether you touched a player recently. If you want a player to matter in your scout stream, keep meaningful time on that player.

# Capacity matters

Free users and premium users do not have the same total scout capacity. Before moving scouts, think about whether you are making a small signal change or a full reallocation.

# What a good scout setup looks like

- focused on a few conviction names
- reviewed when game slates or news shift
- not left stale for long periods
- aligned with the rest of your portfolio instead of working against it

# Common mistake

Many users over-fragment their scouts. A thin spread often produces weak outcomes and low clarity. Concentrated conviction is usually easier to evaluate.
