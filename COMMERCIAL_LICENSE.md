# Sportfolio Commercial Licensing

The public Sportfolio source license does not grant commercial-use rights.

A person or organization that wants to use, reproduce, modify, distribute, host, sell, sublicense, integrate, or otherwise exploit Sportfolio for a commercial purpose must first obtain a separate written commercial license from the copyright holder.

Commercial terms may be structured as a fixed fee, recurring license fee, usage-based fee, revenue share, royalty, or another negotiated arrangement. No particular rate, percentage, or approval is offered merely by publishing this repository. Commercial rights exist only after both parties execute a written agreement.

To discuss commercial licensing, contact the repository owner privately through the contact information on the owner's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing confidential business information.

## Historical Versions

Earlier Sportfolio versions were publicly described as MIT-licensed in repository metadata and documentation. This licensing change is prospective. It governs original Sportfolio material first distributed with the current `LICENSE` notice and does not purport to revoke rights that a recipient validly received for an earlier version under earlier terms.

## Contributions and Ownership

Commercial dual licensing requires a clear copyright chain. External code contributions are not accepted unless the contributor completes the contribution terms requested by the maintainer. See `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## No Trademark License

A commercial source-code license does not automatically grant rights to use the Sportfolio name, logos, domains, app-store listings, or other branding. Those rights must be addressed separately in writing.

This file describes the project's licensing policy and is not a substitute for legal advice. A qualified intellectual-property attorney should review the final commercial agreement, ownership history, and any revenue-share terms before the first commercial license is executed.
